Cookie Policy

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function and can be used to understand how visitors use a site.

2. Cookies we use

This website does not currently set any analytics or marketing cookies. If that changes in future — for example, if we enable website analytics — this policy will be updated to list each cookie, its purpose and its duration.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of this website function.

4. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@madeforoccasions.co.uk.
