How It Works

From first enquiry to collection, booking a light-up number or letter should feel effortless. Here's exactly how it works.

01

Choose Your Numbers or Letters

Browse our full inventory of illuminated numbers, or choose from our standard word set — BABY, BRIDE, MR & MRS, LOVE, ENGAGED — plus custom wording. A milestone birthday, an anniversary year or any date or word that matters to you.

02

Send Your Details

Tell us your event date, venue name and postcode through our Check Availability form. This lets us confirm availability, and whether collection or delivery works best for you.

03

Secure Your Booking

Once we've confirmed availability, your date is secured once the required booking payment has been made. We'll walk you through exactly what's needed.

04

We Handle The Rest

Collect and return your hire yourself, or let us deliver it — as part of our delivery service, we'll position and check it at your venue, then return to collect it afterwards.
