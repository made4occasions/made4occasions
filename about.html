Our story

Made for the occasions people remember.

Made for Occasions was created around a simple idea: event décor should look impressive without making organising an event more complicated.

We create statement hire pieces designed to photograph beautifully, feel professionally made and arrive ready for your celebration.

Starting with our signature light-up numbers — and now growing to include illuminated letters — our aim is to build a collection of occasion pieces for birthdays, weddings and celebrations across Lancashire.
