Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Made for Occasions ("we", "us", "our") provides light-up number and letter hire across Blackpool and Lancashire. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you use this website or make an enquiry.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Event details, such as your event date, venue name, postcode and number or wording combination.
  • Technical information collected automatically, such as pages visited and general device information, where analytics tools are enabled (see Cookie Policy).

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote.
  • To arrange collection, delivery, setup and return for confirmed bookings.
  • To communicate with you about your booking.

We do not send marketing communications unless you've asked us to, and you can opt out at any time by getting in touch.

4. Sharing your information

Enquiry form submissions are processed by a third-party form-delivery service in order to send them to our inbox by email. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else for marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver a confirmed booking, and meet our own record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have rights including access to, correction of, and deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@madeforoccasions.co.uk.
