A natural extension of the range

Illuminated letters are built the same way as our numbers — freestanding, finished in warm-white LED, and designed to be the centrepiece of a room rather than an afterthought. Whether it's announcing an engagement, welcoming a new arrival or dressing a wedding top table, they're made to photograph as beautifully as they look in person.
